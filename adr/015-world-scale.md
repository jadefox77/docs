# ADR-015: Compact, dense districts with a big-world illusion

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-01

## Context
City-wide open worlds are made by large teams.

## Decision
Part 1 has 2-3 compact, dense districts (campus, off-campus town, market/downtown) with a big-world illusion. No city-wide open world. One district is fully alive before the next begins.

## Alternatives considered
- **City-wide open world:** pushes Part 1 toward 4-6+ years.

## Consequences
- Level design must use sightlines, distant geometry and transitions to feel larger.

## Revisit if
Part 1 succeeds and a larger city becomes a Part 2 goal.
