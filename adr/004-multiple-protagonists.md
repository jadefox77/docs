# ADR-004: 2-4 protagonists; one player switches between them

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-01

## Context
The story is about several young people under pressure whose choices affect each other.

## Decision
The campaign has two to four protagonists, controlled by one player who switches between them (option A).

## Alternatives considered
- **B, each online player is a protagonist:** ties the story to co-op; not chosen.
- **C, player-created online characters:** still open for the online mode, not the campaign.

## Consequences
- Reputation and save data are tracked per protagonist.
- Missions must handle switching, and consequences can cross from one protagonist to another.
- Scope lever: ship with two protagonists and add more in updates.

## Revisit if
Playtests show switching confuses players or the story works better with one lead.
