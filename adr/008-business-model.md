# ADR-008: Free-to-play with cosmetics and rewarded ads

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-01

## Context
Nigerian players favour free games; upfront prices limit reach.

## Decision
Free-to-play. Revenue from cosmetic purchases and player-chosen rewarded ads. No pay-to-win.

## Alternatives considered
- **Premium (paid upfront):** simpler, but a smaller audience in the main market.
- **Pay-to-win:** damages trust and competitive fairness.

## Consequences
- Earned and purchased currencies are separate; real-money purchases go only through store billing.
- Receipts are verified server-side and every currency change is a ledger row.
- Needs an age rating, privacy policy and clear disclosures.

## Revisit if
Store rules or player feedback make a different model clearly better.
