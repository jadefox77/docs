# ADR-003: Art target: grounded realism, not photorealism

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-01

## Context
Photorealism needs large art teams and strong hardware; the game must run on budget phones and be made by one person.

## Decision
Aim for grounded realism: believable proportions, materials and lighting, consistent enough for one artist to sustain and light enough for budget Android phones.

## Alternatives considered
- **Photorealism:** beyond one developer's capacity and too heavy for target phones.
- **Strong stylisation (toon, low-poly):** easier, but undercuts the serious tone of the story.

## Consequences
- An art bible defines the look before mass production.
- Polygon and texture budgets are set per asset type and tuned on the test phone.

## Revisit if
A team is hired, or the target hardware changes.
