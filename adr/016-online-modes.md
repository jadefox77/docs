# ADR-016: Co-op first, then small competitive modes; battle royale deferred

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-01

## Context
Online modes must reuse campaign systems and stay within one developer's capacity.

## Decision
Online starts with co-op missions for 2-4 players, then small competitive modes (4v4 or 6-8 players, with bots filling empty slots). Battle royale is deferred. Competitive modes are themed around street, heist and survival, not cult rivalry. Exact session sizes stay open until the first networking prototype.

## Alternatives considered
- **Battle royale first:** needs 50-100 players per match and competes head-on with CODM, PUBG Mobile and Free Fire.

## Consequences
- Server authority from the first online mode.
- Cost per player is measured in closed tests before launch.

## Revisit if
The player base proves large enough for bigger modes.
