# ADR-013: Fictional, UNIBEN-inspired campus, playable inside

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-01

## Context
Campus life is central to the opening story.

## Decision
A fictional university inspired by UNIBEN, playable inside (hostels, faculties, lecture theatres, gate, stalls), within a compressed Benin-style town.

## Alternatives considered
- **The real UNIBEN by name:** legal and reputational risk (see ADR-012).
- **Exterior-only campus:** loses much of the story's setting.

## Consequences
- Interiors raise the art workload; reuse modular interior kits.

## Revisit if
Scope pressure forces fewer interiors; cut rooms before cutting the campus.
