# ADR-012: Real locations and brands are fictionalised

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-01

## Context
The game deals with fraud, cultism and corruption; real names create legal and reputational risk.

## Decision
Fictionalise real place names, landmarks and company brands. Never use real cult group names; only hint at them in conversation. Legal review before release.

## Alternatives considered
- **Real names for authenticity:** legal exposure and harm to real people and institutions.

## Consequences
- A naming list is kept with the fictional equivalents.
- Legal review is a release milestone task.

## Revisit if
Legal advice says otherwise.
